Learn how to colour beautifully - Art Docere

Every anime artist should master colour techniques

Mastering colour techniques is essential for creating visually appealing anime art.
Understanding color theory, blending, shading, and lighting will help you bring your characters and scenes to life. Practice regularly and experiment with different color palettes to develop your unique style.

The colour theory is a fundamental aspect of art that every anime artist should understand. It involves the study of how colors interact, the emotional impact of different hues, and the creation of harmonious color schemes. By mastering colour theory, artists can enhance their work's visual appeal and convey specific moods and atmospheres effectively.
