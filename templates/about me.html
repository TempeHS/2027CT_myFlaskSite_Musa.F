About me

Here's a little info about me.

I have made this website because, ever since I was a child, I have been fascinated by anime and manga. I have always been drawn to the unique art style and storytelling techniques used in these mediums. As I grew older, I realized that I wanted to learn how to create my own anime and manga, but I found it difficult to find resources that were both comprehensive and easy to understand. That's why I decided to create this website. I have created this website using the videos that I once watched and learned from. These videos are by a youtuber named "draw like a sir". I really appreciate his work in this industry and really hope I have done justice to his work. I have also added my own personal touch to the website, making it more user-friendly and accessible to beginners. I hope that this website will inspire and empower others to pursue their passion for anime and manga, just as it has inspired me. -Musa faizan (Creator of Art docere)
